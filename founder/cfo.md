## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $50 a month (Frais fixes (donné par Célian) $50).

- **Break-even: 1 séances a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (4 a day): **57%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 6 a day.
